Only generated monochrome bitmap subsets at native 14, 20 and 28 pixel
sizes are distributed. The source OTF and temporary outline/cmap build
artifacts are not part of the repository. The derivative subset name is
"PaperDIY Math". No glyph outlines are rescaled at runtime. Variant
outlines, MATH constants and italic corrections come from the original
OpenType MATH table; they are not guessed from character bounding boxes.

Rebuild with tools/build_math_fonts.py. The checked-in data records Pillow,
FreeType and fontTools versions, source hash, and monochrome threshold.
The initial generator environment used Pillow 12.2.0, FreeType 2.14.3 and
fontTools 4.62.1. Font sources and derived bitmap font data remain under
SIL OFL 1.1; the project MIT license covers its original renderer code.
