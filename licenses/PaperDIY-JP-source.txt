The pinned source contains Japanese regional outlines. The generator applies
hani/JAN locl then vrt2 substitutions, uses vmtx/VORG advances and origins, and
bakes native 24/28 px JIS X 0208 differences plus U+9AD9/U+20BB7 name forms,
and 12/14 px kana/Latin ruby. Tiny cells preserve native descender ink with
at most two extra vertical pixels; no bitmap scaling or clipping is applied.
Identical body glyphs reuse NativeFont. No installed or private font is used.
